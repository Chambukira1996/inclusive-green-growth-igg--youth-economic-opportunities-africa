# Inclusive Green Growth and Youth Economic Opportunities in Africa

## Research Paper

This repository contains the data, Stata code, replication files,
and supplementary materials for the research paper:

"Can Inclusive Green Growth Unlock Economic Opportunities for the Youth
in Africa? Evidence from Labour Market and Income-Based Wealth
Accumulation Outcomes"

## Authors

Madalitso Chambukira
Andrew Jamali
Daniel Mwapasa

National Planning Commission, Malawi

## Study Overview

The study examines the relationship between inclusive green growth
and youth economic opportunities across 54 African countries over
1999–2023.

The analysis focuses on:
- Youth unemployment
- Youth NEET
- Youth income and wealth accumulation

## Data

The repository contains the datasets used in the empirical analysis,
subject to the terms and conditions of the original data providers.

## Replication

The Stata do-files reproduce the empirical analysis reported in the
research paper.
